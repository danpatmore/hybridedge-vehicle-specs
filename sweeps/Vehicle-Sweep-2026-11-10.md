# Vehicle database sweep, 10 Nov 2026

*Copied from the HybridEdge app repo on 22 Nov 2026 as the starting to-do list for the scheduled sweeps. Short commit ids like `0a53afb0` are in that private repo. Since this report: v1.34.0 added the AMG, Bentley, Ferrari and McLaren cars with blanks, and v1.34.1 set `petrolFromFuelFlow` on the B5 rows.*

A research pass for plug-in hybrid (PHEV) and range-extender (REEV) models on sale, priced or dated in Australia that are not in `phev-specs-au.json` (v1.31.0, 126 rows), six weeks after the 1 Oct sweep (`Vehicle-Sweep-2026-10-01.md`). Read-only: nothing was added to the database. Adding rows is the developer's call, and a specs push reaches every tester.

**How it was done.** Four research passes split by brand group (BYD, GWM, Geely and other Chinese brands; the Chery group, MG, JAC and Forthing; mainstream makers; prestige makers), about 35 web searches each, plus a fifth pass checking the file against the FCAI H1 2026 PHEV sales list. No PDF was fetched or downloaded. Most figures come from the press (CarExpert, EV Central, The Beep, Chasing Cars, 4x4 Australia) and maker AU pages where they could be read. "Single" means one outlet only. "Snippet" means a search result summary, page not read. BOC = before on-road costs, d/a = drive-away.

**What a row needs (as of the sweep).** `batteryChemistry` (lfp, nmc or nca), `batteryGrossKWh`, `batteryUsableKWh`, battery warranty years and km, AC charger kW, phases and plug, engine cc and cylinders, body type (suv, wagon, sedan, ute, mpv, hatch, coupe; no van yet) and drive (awd, fwd, rwd). A row missing one is skipped by the app on its own; the rest of the list still loads (`FailableDecodable` in `SpecResponse`, since July). The 1 Oct report's "throws away the whole update" was wrong. The file's existing rules are applied below: one unlabelled battery figure goes in as gross and usable with `batteryUsableIsEstimated` true; AC phases follow from the rating (single-phase Type 2 tops out at 7.4 kW, so 6.6 kW is single-phase and 11 kW is three-phase); a warranty with no km limit is stored as 0 km; AU AC plugs are Type 2.

**Why chemistry blocks rows, and why it doesn't need to (checked in the app code, 10 Nov).** The app never reads `batteryChemistry`, the battery warranty, the AC plug type or `estimatedHomeChargeHours`: no costing, no screen. The AC charger kW and phases appear only on the Garage screen. They were required only because the decoder declared them non-optional. A row with one blank is skipped by itself (no build discards the whole list), so making them optional lets the cars in section 2 go straight into the main list: builds from 242 show them, and older builds (App Store 1.0.1, tester builds to 241) skip just those rows.

- **Car list v1.32.0** (specs `f782fe1`): added the Geely Starray EM-i Inspire AWD, the Jaecoo J7 SHS-P AWD (Summit AWD / Black Edition) and the J7 SHS-P 2WD for 2027; RAV4 FWD 121 km WLTP and AWD 144 km NEDC; Tiggo 7 and J7 (2026) at the makers' 18.4 kWh. - **The XPeng L03 was not ready after all:** engine cc is required too and nobody publishes it. It went in with v1.33.0.
- **App (next build, through Codex):** chemistry, battery warranty, AC rating, phases, plug, home-charge hours and engine cc and cylinders are optional (`0a53afb0`). A download can't blank a built-in car's value; the Garage shows a dash for an unpublished rating or engine; the engine-fuel estimate stands down without a displacement; DC shows a dash, not "None", for DC at an unpublished rate (`0e56e647`). Tests `SpecOptionalFieldsTests` (8), breaks O1 to O4 caught; full suite 3,527 / 11 / 0.
- **Car list v1.33.0** (specs `24b427f`): added the GWM Cannon Hi4-T and Haval H7 (no AC rating, no engine cc), the Mercedes GLE 450e SUV and Coupé (no chemistry, no AU battery warranty, no AU tank or kerb) and the XPeng L03 Power-X (no engine cc). 134 rows; builds before 242 skip exactly these five. Both versions committed, NOT pushed.
- **Caveats:** a car saved with a blank in one of these fields can't be read by a build before 242. So a tester who picks one of the five on 242 and then installs App Store 1.0.1 loses that car's garage record, and a household partner on an older build misses trips from it (each synced trip carries the car's record). Both go away once 1.0.2 is in the App Store.
- **Still held:** the AMG, Bentley, Ferrari and McLaren cars (could now go in with blanks), the A6 allroad (not here until H1 2027), the BYD M9 Dynamic (no AC rating, no EV range; later), the Chery Stockman (unpriced, Feb 2027).
